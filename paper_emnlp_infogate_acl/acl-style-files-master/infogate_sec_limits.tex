\textbf{Scope.}
We evaluate complete-modality inputs only; robustness to missing or corrupted
modalities is future work.

\textbf{Statistical power.}
Table~\ref{tab:lit} reports single-seed optima; variance across seeds and GPUs is
not yet fully characterized.

\textbf{Baselines.}
Literature aggregates may use slightly different splits or preprocessing; head-to-
head comparisons under a unified codebase are incomplete.

\textbf{Reproducibility.}
Large encoders and long Optuna sweeps are compute-intensive; we will release
random seeds, exact CLI, and database URIs upon acceptance.

\textbf{Hyperparameter interaction.}
Many knobs (Gumbel schedule, EMA start, IB weights, selector temperature) interact
non-linearly; our Optuna studies partially explore this space but cannot claim
global optimality.

\textbf{Language coverage.}
Experiments focus on English MOSI/MOSEI and Chinese CH-SIMS v2; other languages
and cultural display norms are unstudied.

\textbf{Oracle metrics.}
Training logs optionally record ``oracle'' test-MAE epochs for debugging; these
are \emph{not} used for model selection and should not be quoted as primary
scores.
